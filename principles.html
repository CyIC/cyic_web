What this looks like in a meeting

A member brings a company they have researched on the club’s standard worksheet, circulated beforehand so nobody is reading it cold. They make the case. The room pushes back: on the growth assumption, on the price, on whether we already own something too similar.

Then we vote — one vote each by default, though any partner may call for a capital-weighted retally — and the reasoning is written down whichever way it goes. Sometimes the presenter is sent away to come back with more. That is not a failure of the process; it is the process.
