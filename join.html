This is not an offer of membership

The club does not advertise, solicit, or accept unsolicited applications for membership, and nothing on this site is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy an interest in the club or any security. An interest in the club is not transferable, is not registered with any securities regulator, and is not available to the public.

The club is published here because its members believe a club should be able to show its workings. Read it as a record of how we operate.

What a partner commits

Money
{{ site.club.minimum_monthly }} / month minimum

The club charges nothing to join and nothing to withdraw funds. The floor is low because a club should be affordable rather than exclusive.

Time
~2 hours / month

Plus research time during the month. Falling short of a partner’s duties for three consecutive months is grounds for removal, which keeps the club real.

Participation
One vote each, by default

Any partner may ask for a vote to be retallied weighted by capital account, and competing stock proposals are ranked on a point ballot.

Learning
Onboarding training

Partners are trained on the stock worksheet before being asked to present one. Experience is not a prerequisite; participation is.

What the club is not

  • Not a fund. Nobody can hand the club money to manage. Every partner is required to take an active part in researching and voting on the club’s holdings, which is precisely what keeps a club a club under the SEC’s guidance on investment clubs.
  • Not a broker or adviser. No partner is licensed, no partner is paid for advice, and nothing published here is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
  • Not passive. Anyone who wants to set it and forget it is better served by an index fund, and the club will say so cheerfully.
  • Not open. {{ site.club.member_cap }} partners is the cap, admission is unanimous, and membership is not offered publicly. Starting a club is genuinely not difficult, and the resources page lists what we used to start this one.
