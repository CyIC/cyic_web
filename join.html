What you are committing to

Money
{{ site.club.minimum_monthly }} / month minimum

No fee to join and no fee to withdraw your funds. Hardship exceptions are available — just ask.

Time
~2 hours / month

Plus research time during the month. Falling short of a partner’s duties for three consecutive months is grounds for removal, which keeps the club real.

Participation
One vote each, by default

Any partner may ask for a vote to be retallied weighted by capital account, and competing stock proposals are ranked on a point ballot.

Learning
Onboarding training

You will be trained on the stock worksheet before you are asked to present one. Beginners are genuinely welcome.

What the club is not

  • Not a fund. You cannot hand us money to manage. Investment clubs operate under specific SEC rules that require members to participate, so we are not open to the public as an investment vehicle.
  • Not a broker or adviser. Nobody here is licensed, and nothing we publish is investment advice.
  • Not passive. If you want to set it and forget it, an index fund will serve you better and we will say so cheerfully.
  • Not unlimited. {{ site.club.member_cap }} members is the cap. If we are full, we will help you start your own club.
